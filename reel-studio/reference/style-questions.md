# Style questions

Ask these after they've named their favourite creators, one at a time. Offer the choices as short sketches, plus "something else" and "you decide". If they say "you decide", decide from their frame picks later and tell them what you chose. There are **no defaults from anyone else's taste**: every answer is theirs.

Save answers in `profile.json → preferences` with these keys.

| Key | Question (plain words) | Choices to offer |
|---|---|---|
| `framing` | "How should the camera move?" | `zoom-to-side`: punch in and move you to one side, graphics fill the other side · `wide`: you stay in the middle, graphics appear around or over you · `mix` · `still`: no camera moves at all |
| `face` | "Can graphics ever cover your face?" | `never` · `briefly` (quick pop-ups are fine) · `yes` |
| `fullscreen` | "Can a graphic fill the whole screen for a moment (you disappear)?" | `never` · `rarely` (1–2 short moments) · `often` |
| `change_every` | "How busy should it feel?" | `calm` (something new every 4–6 s) · `steady` (every 2–4 s) · `fast` (every 1–2 s) |
| `cut` | "How tight should the cut be?" | `natural` (keeps your breathing room) · `tight` (pauses removed) |
| `speed` | "Should the final video be slightly sped up?" | `1.0` · `1.1` · `1.15` · `1.2` |
| `captions` | "Captions?" | `none` · `words` (every word, 3 at a time) · `key` (only key words, as part of the graphics) |
| `text_case` | "Big text in CAPITALS or normal?" | `upper` · `normal` |
| `colour` | "How much colour in the graphics?" | `minimal` (black/white + one accent) · `brand` (your brand colours) · `bold` |
| `cards` | "What kind of boxes for text and pictures?" | `solid` (clean white cards) · `glass` (dark see-through) · `none` (just text) |
| `pictures` | "When you mention a tool, a person or a number, should a picture of it appear?" | `always` · `sometimes` · `rarely` |
| `music` | "Music under your voice?" | `none` · a mood in their words (calm, upbeat, lo-fi, cinematic…) |
| `colour_grade` | (only for normal phone/camera video) "Any colour look on the video itself?" | `none` (as filmed) · `a bit more contrast` |

After the frame picker, compare what they said here with what they picked. If they clash (said "never cover the face" but said yes to frames with text over faces), ask about it in one sentence. Their latest answer wins.
